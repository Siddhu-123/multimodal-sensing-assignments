## 2. Executive Summary and Context

Replace the current text under **Project Description** with:

> SA Water’s Smart Infrastructure Program includes projects that support monitoring, failure prevention, early fault detection and maintenance planning across water and wastewater networks. This project focuses on the potential placement of sensors in wastewater networks to support earlier identification of abnormal sewer-level conditions and potential overflow risks.
>
> Current sensor-placement decisions are understood to draw on information such as historical failures, customer complaints and proximity to environmentally sensitive locations. The project will confirm the current decision process with SA Water and identify the data and criteria needed to make placement decisions more systematic and repeatable.
>
> The project will develop an evidence-informed methodology for wastewater sensor placement. It will review relevant sensor technologies and sewer failure mechanisms, catalogue the parameters that may influence placement, and develop a prototype method for ranking candidate locations and estimating sensor density for an agreed pilot area. The method will be demonstrated through two agreed scenarios: a baseline with no sensors deployed and a scenario that includes existing sensors. The outputs will be documented with their assumptions, data requirements and limitations so that SA Water can determine whether and how the method should be extended.
>
> The project is scheduled to conclude by 30 November 2026. Final outputs will be submitted to SA Water and the academic project team in accordance with the approved roadmap and acceptance requirements.

Replace **Project Purpose/Justification** with:

> Wastewater overflows can create operational, environmental, financial and reputational consequences. Earlier identification of abnormal sewer-level conditions may give operators more time to investigate and respond, but the value of monitoring depends on whether sensors are located where they provide useful network coverage and risk information.
>
> This project addresses the absence of a documented, repeatable basis for comparing candidate sensor locations and deployment densities. It will provide a transparent decision-support method rather than an operational guarantee. The method will combine relevant network, asset, consequence and environmental factors, subject to data availability and SA Water’s review. A pilot demonstration will show how the method works, identify its limitations and support a decision about future development.
